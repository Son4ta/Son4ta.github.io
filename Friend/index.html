Friends & peers

Friends from my academic journey. These introductions reflect the affiliations and interests recorded when this page was first written.

XS

Xiaoyu Sun

Stevens Institute of Technology

Xiaoyu Sun studied Intelligent Science and Technology at Wuhan Institute of Technology, where he was recognized as an outstanding graduate of the class of 2024, before pursuing a master’s degree in Computer Science at Stevens Institute of Technology. His experience includes a data analyst role at Bank of Communications in Urumqi, work in artificial intelligence and image processing, and awards in national robotics and AI competitions.

Research interests: 3D reconstruction · Reinforcement learning · Computer vision

ZZ

Zixiong Zhang

Central China Normal University

Zixiong Zhang studied Software Engineering at Wuhan Institute of Technology before pursuing a master’s degree in Computer Science and Technology at Central China Normal University. His experience includes leading a project at the Hubei Engineering Research Center for Intelligent Production-Line Equipment.

He also creates videos in his spare time; the original introduction recorded 366,000 views on Bilibili. His achievements include national awards in mathematics, algorithm design, robotics, and artificial intelligence, as well as one published patent application.

Research interests: Convex optimization · Reinforcement learning · Random networks

JY

Jiangqi Ye

Beijing Electronic Science and Technology Institute

Jiangqi Ye was an undergraduate at Wuhan Institute of Technology with a recommendation-based offer for master’s study at Beijing Electronic Science and Technology Institute when this introduction was written. He has worked in business development at China Unicom and is an enthusiastic practitioner of digital media, film effects, and video post-production.

Research interests: Software engineering · Digital media technology

Beyond the technical work: a devoted fan of Running Man.

ZS

Ziyu Sun

University of Chinese Academy of Sciences

Ziyu Sun graduated with distinction in Internet of Things Engineering from the School of Cyber Science and Engineering at Zhengzhou University before pursuing a master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. A debater from Zhoukou, Henan, he built a strong foundation in operating systems and data structures and hopes to contribute to computer-science research through further study.

Research interests: Internet of Things · Embedded intelligent systems · Distributed computing

KY

Kaizhan Yang

China University of Geosciences

Kaizhan Yang studied Intelligent Science and Technology at Wuhan Institute of Technology, graduating with distinction in 2024, before pursuing a master’s degree in Computer Science at China University of Geosciences, Wuhan. Originally from Shangqiu, Henan, he developed a strong undergraduate foundation in calculus, probability, mathematical statistics, and linear algebra.

Outside research, he is known among friends for his enthusiasm for Valorant and Soulslike games—and for holding his own at a celebration.

Research interests: Parallel computing · Computer architecture · CUDA

ZT

Zhijiang Tang

University of Chinese Academy of Sciences

Zhijiang Tang studied Data Science and Big Data Technology at Nankai University and was named an outstanding graduate of the class of 2024 before pursuing a master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences.

As an undergraduate, he led a research project on deep learning for pediatric vascular imaging. The project entered Nankai University’s 2022 National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program and received a National Second Prize in the 18th Challenge Cup National College Student Extracurricular Academic Science and Technology Competition. His awards also include a National Second Prize in the 2022 Contemporary Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling and a First Prize in the 2022 Asia and Pacific Mathematical Contest in Modeling.

His research publications include “Radiation reduction for interventional radiology imaging: a video frame interpolation solution”.

Research interests: Time-series forecasting · Medical image analysis · AI for science

YT

Ye Tao

Huazhong University of Science and Technology

Ye Tao was an undergraduate at Wuhan Institute of Technology preparing to begin a master’s degree at Huazhong University of Science and Technology when this introduction was written. He hopes to study robotics during graduate school, with an interest in robot security.

Away from research, his introduction reads like a gaming résumé: a model citizen of Los Santos, a veteran of Counter-Strike maps including Dust II, Inferno, and Mirage, and a regular visitor to Summoner’s Rift. His plan is to return to those fictional worlds with better robotics skills and the same sense of humor.

Research interests: Robot security
