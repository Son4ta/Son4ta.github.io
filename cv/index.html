Profile

I am a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at the Hangzhou Institute for Advanced Study, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Jungang Xu. My research focuses on scientific machine learning, embodied intelligence, and autonomous research agents. I study how physical structure, task representations, and feedback can inform learning systems, with applications in ocean carbon-flux forecasting, long-horizon navigation, and autonomous experimentation. I expect to graduate in June 2027.
