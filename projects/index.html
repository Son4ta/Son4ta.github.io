03 / Research programs

Research directions

I study learning systems that use physical structure, task representations, and feedback to model the world and act within it.

01

Physics-aware learning / Spatiotemporal modeling

Scientific machine learning

My work on ocean carbon dynamics studies how learning systems can represent the different processes that shape a complex physical system. Carbon-ECS combines transport modeling with local detail refinement. REM-MoE uses hierarchical experts to separate environmental regimes from physical mechanisms.

My current work also investigates how temporal reaction search can be separated from spatial physical response for ocean carbon-flux forecasting.

ICASSP 2026CCF BFirst author

Carbon-ECS: A Benchmark and a Physics-Decoupled Model for the East China Sea Carbon Flux

Chengjie Fang, Enyuan Zhao, Hongming Yao, Ziyu Sun, Di Gao, Yanbiao Li

A 17-year, approximately 1 km benchmark and a physics-inspired model that pair large-scale transport modeling with iterative refinement of local carbon-flux detail.

02

Robot navigation / Reinforcement learning

Embodied intelligence

I study how agents represent task progress, verify intermediate states, and recover from failure in long-horizon navigation. StageNav explores geometric verification and state-aligned reinforcement learning, while ForesightNav combines semantic understanding with geometric exploration.

IROS 2026CCF CFirst authorACCEPTED

Chengjie Fang, Yujia Yang, Yu Liu, Ziyu Sun, Jungang Xu

Geometrically verified task graphs and state-aligned reinforcement learning help navigation agents assess progress, recognize failure, and recover over long horizons.

Earlier work / Undergraduate robotics

Humanoid perception and robotic manipulation

At Wuhan Institute of Technology, I worked with Tongzhou Zhao and Xun Li. I joined the university's robotics laboratory in my second year, working on image processing and control for humanoid robots.

In my third year, I led a robotic-arm project for board-game playing using deep learning and reinforcement learning. The project was entered in national student competitions. These experiences shaped my interest in the connection between perception, decision-making, and robot behavior.
