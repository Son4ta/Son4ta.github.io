During my undergraduate studies in Intelligent Science and Technology at [Wuhan Institute of Technology](https://www.wit.edu.cn/), I worked with [Tongzhou Zhao](https://cs.wit.edu.cn/info/1026/5517.htm) and [Xun Li](https://cs.wit.edu.cn/info/1026/8528.htm).

In my second year, I joined the university's [robotics laboratory](https://robot.wit.edu.cn/) and gained hands-on experience with image processing and control on humanoid robots. In my third year, I led a robotic-arm project for board-game playing using deep learning and reinforcement learning. The project was entered in national student competitions.

These experiences introduced me to the practical interaction between perception, decision-making, and robot behavior, and informed my later work on embodied intelligence.
