# Static decorations

The website retains thin rules, orbital outlines, and restrained brick / amber / teal color strips inspired by the official Lone Trail references in [design-references.md](design-references.md). The header uses the earlier simple F mark.

The assembling and separating background logos, CF orbital monogram, animation controller, and motion controls have been removed. Decorative geometry is static, stays behind content, and does not intercept pointer input. Company and university marks remain low-opacity backgrounds in their respective cards.

This treatment requires no continuously running decorative animation, timers, canvas, or WebGL. Preserve the readable foreground text, full-color portrait, four-size typography system, and access to all content on small screens and older devices.
