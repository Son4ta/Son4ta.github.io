This was the beginning of my literature-search workflow in November 2024. I was trying to investigate quadruped robotics and needed a concrete first step before attempting a full survey.

## Start with the terminology

Before searching for papers, I collected the English terms used to describe the topic. ChatGPT, Tencent Yuanbao, and Youdao helped me move between Chinese descriptions and English search phrases.

My initial keyword list was:

```text
quadrupedal
robots
quadruped
```

Combining them into a phrase such as `quadrupedal robots` gave me an entry point in Google Scholar. From there, I could open a promising paper and inspect the terms its authors used.

## Keep the scope honest

The original notebook stopped after this first search step. It did not yet contain a completed review, a systematic screening protocol, or results from using AI to synthesize the papers. I keep it here as a small record of how I started, alongside the more developed [prompt examples](/blog/2024/prompt/).
