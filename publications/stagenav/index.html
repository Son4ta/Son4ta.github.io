StageNav: A Diagnostic Benchmark and State-Aligned RL Framework for Long-Horizon Navigation

Chengjie Fang, Yujia Yang, Yu Liu, Ziyu Sun, Jungang Xu

READ PAPER ↗BIBTEX ↓

METHOD / FINDINGS

StageNav

Geometrically verified task graphs and state-aligned reinforcement learning help navigation agents assess progress, recognize failure, and recover over long horizons.

StageNav-Bench parses long instructions into geometrically anchored DAGs and evaluates node-level execution and cognitive state alignment. The policy jointly predicts navigation actions and cognitive states (success, failure, or in progress).

59.1% navigation success on R2R-CE. Unseen-validation continuous navigation; this is the paper's reported evaluation result. (Table II, p. 6.)
