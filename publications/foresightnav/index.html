METHOD / FINDINGS

ForesightNav

A training-free navigation framework that combines semantic maps, graph-based target verification, and geometric frontier exploration for object, instance, and text goals.

Context-aware semantic value maps guide broad search while online scene graphs verify fine-grained goal matches. Semantic score dispersion and graph-matching confidence choose among geometric exploration, semantic exploration, partial-match exploration, and target verification.

+18.6 percentage points in RoboTHOR success over UniGoal. RoboTHOR ObjectNav benchmark; percentage-point increase, not relative percent. (Table 1, p. 6.)
